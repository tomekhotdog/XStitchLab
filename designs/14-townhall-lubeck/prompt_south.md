Create a pixel art illustration designed specifically to become a cross-stitch pattern.

Subject: The right half of the south facade (Marktplatz side) of Lübeck Town Hall, Germany. Straight front-on view.

COMPOSITION (top to bottom, four zones):

1. TURRETS AND ROOFLINE (top ~15%):
- 5 slim pointed dark spires along the roofline, varying heights
- Dark green-grey (oxidised copper/slate)
- Between the spires: tall narrow Gothic pointed-arch openings through which sky is visible

2. DARK WALL WITH CIRCULAR MEDALLIONS (~25%):
- VERY DARK BRICK wall — almost black-brown
- A row of 5 large colourful CIRCULAR MEDALLIONS (Windlöcher) evenly spaced
- Each medallion: a decorative shield in warm colours (gold, terracotta, red) against the dark wall
- A band of Gothic decorative tracery runs below the medallions
- This is THE most distinctive feature

3. ARCHED WINDOWS (~30%):
- 3 large pointed Gothic arched windows with white mullioned glass panes
- Set into the same dark brick wall
- Each window has a pointed brick arch above it

4. GROUND-LEVEL ARCADE (~30%):
- A row of tall pointed dark brick arches forming an open arcade
- Dark arch outlines with shadowy interiors

STYLE:
- Charming, slightly stylised pixel art with bold clean shapes
- Dark and Gothic mood — not cheerful or pastel
- No gradients, no anti-aliasing

CROSS-STITCH CONSTRAINTS:
- 40 wide x 50 tall pixel grid
- Maximum 8 flat colours
- Pale blue sky background

COLOUR PALETTE (max 8):
1. Very dark brown-black (brick walls)
2. Dark green-grey (turret roofs)
3. Warm gold/ochre (medallion shields)
4. Terracotta/muted red (medallion accents)
5. White/cream (window panes, details)
6. Medium dark brown (outlines, tracery)
7. Pale sky blue (background)
8. Dark grey (arcade interiors, shadows)

OUTPUT:
- Clean pixel art only
- No people, market stalls, umbrellas, or street furniture
- No text, labels, borders, or mockups
- Do NOT show fabric or embroidery
