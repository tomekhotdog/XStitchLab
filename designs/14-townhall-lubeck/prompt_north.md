Create a pixel art illustration designed specifically to become a cross-stitch pattern.

Subject: The NORTH facade (Breite Straße side) of Lübeck Town Hall, Germany — the iconic dark brick wall with rows of large circular wind holes and decorative turrets along the roofline.

PERSPECTIVE:
- Straight front-on view of the north facade only
- Symmetrical composition, centered on the facade

COMPOSITION (top to bottom):

ROOFLINE:
- Two pointed turrets/spires flanking the facade, dark green-grey (oxidised copper or slate)
- Between them, smaller decorative pinnacles
- The turrets define the silhouette and should rise above the main wall

MAIN WALL:
- A tall wall of VERY DARK BRICK — almost black-brown, dramatically darker than normal red brick
- The wall features CIRCULAR WIND HOLES (Windlöcher) arranged in rows
- TWO ROWS of circles: upper row has larger circles, lower row slightly smaller or same size
- Approximately 4-5 circles per row, evenly spaced
- Circles contain colourful medallion shields — warm gold, terracotta, muted red tones standing out against the dark wall
- The repeating geometric pattern of bright circles on dark brick is THE signature feature

GROUND LEVEL:
- A row of pointed Gothic arches at the base — an open arcade/walkway
- Simplify to 4-5 arches
- Dark outlines with light interiors suggesting the passage behind
- Simple ground line below

KEY DIFFERENCE FROM SOUTH FACADE:
- The north side is MORE PURELY GOTHIC — no Renaissance white loggia, no green copper canopy
- It is darker, more austere, more repetitive in its geometry
- The circles-on-dark-brick pattern dominates almost the entire facade

STYLE:
- Charming, slightly stylised pixel art
- Bold, clean shapes
- Strong dark outlines
- No gradients, no lighting effects, no anti-aliasing
- Dark, imposing, Gothic mood

CROSS-STITCH CONSTRAINTS:
- Design must fit within a 40 x 45 stitch grid (40 wide, 45 tall)
- Maximum 8 colours
- Flat colour blocks only
- Simple pale blue sky background

COLOUR PALETTE (max 8):
1. Very dark brown-black (main wall brick — VERY dark, almost black)
2. Dark green-grey (turret roofs, pinnacles)
3. Warm gold / ochre (medallion shields in circles)
4. Terracotta / muted red (medallion variation)
5. Cream / light stone (arch interiors, occasional trim)
6. Medium dark brown (outlines, arch frames)
7. Pale sky blue (background)
8. Warm grey (ground line, depth accents)

OUTPUT:
- Clean pixel art only, centered symmetrical composition
- No people, street furniture, or surrounding buildings
- No text, labels, borders, or mockups
- Do NOT show fabric or embroidery
