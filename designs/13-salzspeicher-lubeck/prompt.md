Create pixel art for a cross-stitch pattern of the Salzspeicher (salt warehouses) in Lubeck, Germany.

SUBJECT:
- Front-facing view of exactly 3 of the historic brick gabled warehouses, tightly packed side-by-side
- Daytime scene, plain pale blue sky background
- Buildings fill most of frame width, ~two-thirds of frame height
- Below the buildings: a thin strip of green (grass/trees along the riverbank), then a strip of muted blue-grey water at the very bottom — the classic waterfront view

KEY FEATURES:
- Mix of stepped gables and pointed gables at varying heights — this variety defines the Salzspeicher
- Each building has its own distinct facade colour, but use two alternating shades (e.g. light and dark brick red) within each facade to suggest brick texture — interleave rows or columns of the two shades
- Adjacent buildings should be clearly different colours from each other
- Small dark rectangular windows in 2-3 rows per building
- Simple doors/loading bays at ground level
- Dark brown outlines (not black)

STYLE: Cute, charming pixel art. Bold clean shapes, flat colours, no gradients or anti-aliasing. Cross-stitch readable.

CONSTRAINTS:
- 40 x 40 pixel grid
- Max 10 colours: two shades of brick red, two shades of warm brown, dark brown (outlines/windows), light beige, pale sky blue, muted green (riverbank), muted blue-grey (water), warm grey
- White Aida fabric shows through as sky

OUTPUT: Clean pixel art only. Centered. No people, boats, text, labels, borders, mockups, fabric, or embroidery.
