Artus Court is a grand Gothic hall on the Long Market in Gdańsk, Poland — built in the 14th century as a meeting place for the city's merchants and named after the legendary King Arthur. Its ornate Renaissance facade, with tall arched windows and a stepped parapet, reflects the wealth of a city that was one of the Hanseatic League's most important Baltic ports. Inside, the Great Hall housed lavish banquets and civic gatherings beneath what was once the tallest brick vault in medieval Europe.
