3 Hampshire Rise
The Story
3 Hampshire Rise is a semi-detached residence of distinguished character in the rolling suburbs of southern England — a property that has, against all odds and after decades of faithful monthly tribute to the bank, finally been liberated from the shackles of its mortgage. Built in the optimistic style of the late 20th century, it features an attic once shared with a resident squirrel, and a driveway that has witnessed the full lifecycle of at least four family cars. The front garden, once home to a tree of considerable stature, now enjoys unobstructed sunlight — thanks to a legendary act of felling that the neighbours have not yet forgotten. Home to the Szymaniec family of five, the house has stoically endured school runs, Christmas dinners, and an ambitious programme of DIY improvements. Rooms have been reorganised, walls have disappeared, shelves fitted, and every remaining surface painted and repainted until exactly right. Now mortgage-free, it stands as a monument to patience, persistence, and the quiet satisfaction of a job well done. Congratulations are in order — Barclays will miss you terribly.
